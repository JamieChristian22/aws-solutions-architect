# FinOps Playbook

1. Allocate: mandatory Environment, Application, Owner, CostCenter, ManagedBy.
2. Detect: budgets and anomaly detection.
3. Optimize: rightsizing, schedules, lifecycle, Graviton, commitments after usage stabilizes.
4. Govern: monthly review with Finance + engineering owners.
5. Measure: savings realized, utilization, forecast variance, unallocated spend.

## KPIs
Tagged spend ≥98%  
Budget forecast variance <10%  
Unallocated spend <2%  
Optimization backlog aging <60 days
