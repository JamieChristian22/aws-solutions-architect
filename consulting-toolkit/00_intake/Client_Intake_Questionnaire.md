# Client Intake Questionnaire

## Business
- What business outcome must this project enable?
- What is the target launch date?
- What is the measurable definition of success?
- Which applications/workloads are in scope?
- What business processes are blocked or degraded today?

## Technical
- Current hosting model and regions?
- Expected user/request/data growth over 12–24 months?
- Current database engines and storage volumes?
- Existing CI/CD and IaC tools?
- Current monitoring, backup, DR, IAM, and network patterns?

## Security / Compliance
- Data classification?
- Regulatory obligations?
- Required encryption standards?
- Identity provider?
- Log retention?
- Vulnerability-management process?

## Operations
- Support hours?
- RTO/RPO?
- Availability objective?
- Change approval model?
- Incident severity model?

## FinOps
- Current monthly cloud spend?
- Budget owner?
- Cost allocation tags?
- Savings Plans / RIs?
- Monthly review process?
