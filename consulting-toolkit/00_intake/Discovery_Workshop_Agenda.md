# Discovery Workshop — 90 Minutes

0–10 min: business outcomes and scope  
10–25 min: current-state architecture  
25–40 min: applications/data/integrations  
40–55 min: security/compliance  
55–65 min: reliability/DR/operations  
65–75 min: cost/FinOps  
75–85 min: constraints, assumptions, dependencies  
85–90 min: decisions, actions, next steps

## Exit Criteria
- business outcome documented
- in/out-of-scope agreed
- RTO/RPO/availability targets captured
- security constraints captured
- top five risks identified
- assessment evidence list agreed
